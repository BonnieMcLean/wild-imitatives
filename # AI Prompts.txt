# AI Prompts

ChatGPT was used to generate the images of the animals in a consistent way across species.

## Prompt used for generation of dog images:

Create a photorealistic, full-body image of an adult [DOG BREED], standing naturally in a strict side profile and facing right.

Show the entire dog, including all four paws, ears and tail. Use accurate breed-specific anatomy, proportions, coat texture, markings and colouring.

The dog stands on a perfectly level, horizontal ground plane against a pure white, evenly lit studio background. Use a neutral eye-level camera angle, natural proportions, soft realistic shadows and consistent studio lighting.

Keep the camera perspective neutral, with no wide-angle distortion. Do not enlarge the dog to fill the frame. Leave generous white space around the subject. No collar, leash, people, furniture, text, labels, borders or other objects.

The image will be used in a collection of separate dog-breed images calibrated to a shared scale based on shoulder height. Prioritise realistic anatomy and a clear, unobstructed view of the dog's withers and feet.

-----

For the Rottweiler, I edited this to ask to show it growling.


## Prompt used for generation of other animals:

Create a photorealistic, full-body image of an adult [ANIMAL], standing naturally in a strict side profile and facing right.

Show the entire animal. Use accurate anatomy, proportions, skin texture, markings and colouring.

The animal stands on a perfectly level, horizontal ground plane against a pure white, evenly lit studio background. Use a neutral eye-level camera angle, natural proportions, soft realistic shadows and consistent studio lighting.

Keep the camera perspective neutral, with no wide-angle distortion. Do not enlarge the animal to fill the frame. Leave generous white space around the animal. No collar, leash, people, furniture, text, labels, borders or other objects.

The image will be used in a collection of separate images calibrated to a shared scale based on shoulder height. Prioritise realistic anatomy and a clear, unobstructed view of the animal's entire body.

-----

For the Rottweiler, German Shepherd, and the wolf, I edited the prompt to ask to show it growling.

For the Whistling Kite and the frogs, I provided reference images of the real animals.
